Glossary of Terms

APY
Annual Percentage Yield — Total interest earned over one year, including compounding effects.
APR
Annual Percentage Rate — Annualized interest rate charged on loans or credit card balances.
NCUA
National Credit Union Administration — U.S. government agency that insures credit union deposits up to $250,000.
FDIC
Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation — U.S. government agency that insures bank deposits up to $250,000.
FinCEN
Financial Crimes Enforcement Network — U.S. Treasury bureau that collects financial transaction reports.
FOM
Field of Membership — Eligibility requirements for joining a credit union.
FBAR
Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts — IRS form required for foreign financial account reporting.
FATCA
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act — U.S. law requiring foreign financial institutions to report accounts held by U.S. persons.
NSF
Non-Sufficient Funds — Fee charged when a transaction exceeds available balance.
CO-OP Shared Branching
Network allowing credit union members to conduct transactions at other participating credit union branches nationwide.

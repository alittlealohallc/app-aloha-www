Why Aloha?

A Little Aloha LLC is a single-person IT consulting practice run by Kent Schaeffer out of West Valley City, Utah. Before going independent, Kent spent 25+ years in enterprise infrastructure — including DNS, DHCP, and IPAM work for organizations like Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Fidelity, Five9, Con Edison, and Avnet.

That same rigor now goes toward small businesses, home offices, and solo practitioners who need their technology to just work — without an enterprise contract or an enterprise price tag. Every engagement is flat-fee, scoped up front, and handed over fully documented. You own what gets built: the code and the documentation are always yours, even when hosting runs on Cloudflare.

Aloha shows up here as genuine care in the work — not palm trees or tiki decor. This is a serious engineering practice with island warmth in how it treats clients.

  • Remote service: hosting, DNS, code, and docs — nationwide, no radius limit
  • Consultation: always free, virtual or on-site
